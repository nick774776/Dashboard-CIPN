CANCER SURVIVAL RISK
CIPN-driven dose reductions in curative-intent adjuvant therapy can reduce efficacy and negatively affect cancer survival outcomes in breast and colorectal cancer.
FALL RISK
CIPN is a significant contributor to fall risk in hospitalized patients undergoing chemotherapy, identified as an independent factor in multivariate logistic models (Japan, n=21,717).
PSYCHOLOGICAL
CIPN is independently associated with depression, anxiety, insomnia, reduced physical wellbeing, and impaired fine motor function, compounding cancer survivorship burden.
